## Bundled wallpaper images

The three wallpapers in `app/src/main/assets/wallpapers/` are original abstract landscapes created for Ease TV. Their editable SVG sources are in `artwork/`; these assets are included under this project's MIT licence.

The development TV previously used owner-supplied Wallhaven files with the same friendly labels. Those original images are not included in this repository or its release APK. Updating the development TV preserves its existing private wallpaper copies; fresh installations receive the abstract landscapes.
